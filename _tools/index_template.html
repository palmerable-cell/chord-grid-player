__ABOUT__

  • Open a song, press Play: the original video plays in the small YouTube player and the chart shows where you are. The highlighted bar moves to each chord 1 second before it is played (adjustable with “Marker lead”).
  • Click any bar to jump there · Loop a passage (A/B buttons or “Loop cells…”) · slow down to 75 % or 50 % (YouTube keeps the pitch) · keyboard: space, ←/→, [ ], L.
  • Each song page links to the original video. Charts are shown exactly as published (licence CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: attribution, non-commercial, no derivatives). Bar timings were tracked from the moving highlight in the videos and aligned to the beats in the audio; songs marked “estimated” follow the tempo only.
